# Privacy Policy

_Effective September 29, 2026_

Code Practice ("the app") is a free, open-source practice site. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

The app has **no accounts, no server-side database, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies**. Everything you do stays in your own browser.

## What is stored, and where

All data is kept **only on your device**, in your browser's local storage:

- **Your code** for each problem and the playground.
- **Your progress**: which problems you attempted or solved, attempt counts, and timestamps (used for the streak counter).
- **Your settings**, such as theme, font size and editor preferences.
- **Temporary list filters**, kept in session storage and cleared when you close the tab.

The app also uses a service worker to cache its own files, including the Python runtime, so it works offline.

None of this is sent to the app's author or anyone else. Your code runs in your browser via WebAssembly (Pyodide) and is never uploaded.

## Hosting

The app is served as static files by **GitHub Pages**. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details when it delivers the site. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app itself loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

## Export, import and deletion

- **Export** (Settings → Your data) downloads a backup file to your device. What you do with that file is up to you.
- **Reset** (Settings → Your data) erases your progress and saved code from this device.
- Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes everything, including the offline cache.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. The history of changes is public in the project's source repository.
